About Us

Our Company

TravelBloom is a travel recommendation company founded with one mission: to help travellers find destinations that match their dreams. We curate the world's most beautiful beaches, historic temples and exciting countries, and present them in a simple, searchable way.

Our team combines local knowledge with years of travel experience to bring you honest, inspiring recommendations for every kind of trip.

Guidance for Team Members

  • Always verify destination information and images before publishing a recommendation.
  • Respond to customer enquiries from the Contact Us page within 24 hours.
  • Respect local cultures and promote responsible, sustainable tourism.
  • Keep the recommendation data (JSON) up to date and report broken links or images.
  • Collaborate openly and share new destination ideas with the team every week.
